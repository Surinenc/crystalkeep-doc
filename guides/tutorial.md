# The Initiation

The Tutorial entry runs nine lessons. They are gated on **doing**, not on
reading — a lesson completes when you demonstrate the thing, and repeats when
you do not.

Selecting Tutorial does not hijack Continue or New Siege. It is its own entry.

## The nine lessons

**1 — The Warden.** Move, jump, dash, and commit sword attacks. Health falls
from damage; regenerating Ward fuels the dash and, later, the spells.

**2 — Read the siege.** Open the tactical map, inspect the announced gate, and
walk its cyan route back to the crystal. Only the announced gate attacks next;
other portal routes are inactive. The route ends at the crystal — step into its
sanctuary. *Crystal destruction loses the siege; Warden defeat does not.*

**3 — Build and aim.** Place a Blockade on the marker, then a Ballista: lock
its location first, aim its rotation second. The range and dead-zone preview
rotate before mana is committed. Then sell the practice Blockade and read the
refund — **60% of total mana invested**.

**4 — Start the wave.** You start every normal wave. Unspent setup time is
yours to use or waste.

**5 — Change the keep.** Approach a Counterweight Bridge, read its banner
before touching it, then raise it. The route changes. Open the map, find the
refreshed bypass, and cover it with a new Ballista.

> A topology change is a redeployment problem, not free damage.

**6 — Frontal shield law.** Watch a Ballista strike a Shieldbearer's front and
read the zero. *Frontal Pierce is always blocked, regardless of item or tower
level.* Then break the rule correctly — a Cannon, or an attack from the flank.

**7 — Fire Demon law.** Watch a Cannon shell dissipate against a Fire Demon.
*Cannon damage is always zero against a Fire Demon.* Place a Frost Rune on its
route to chill it, then finish it with a valid damage source. Control creates
time and combinations; it is not damage by itself.

**8 — Warden intervention.** Mark a Sapper with Hex, intercept it yourself, and
stabilize the damaged defense with Mend or a repair. All three actions are
required. *The Warden saves weak points; the Warden does not replace the plan.*

**9 — Claim the reward.** Approach the dropped item, inspect it, choose Loot,
then equip it. Unclaimed floor loot is never collected automatically. Rarity
controls stat count; item level controls roll range.

## Why lessons 6 and 7 exist

They teach the two hard-zero laws, and they are the reason the Initiation is
worth playing even if you know the genre. A player who has not met these laws
loses a run without understanding what happened — the defense was fine, the
positioning was fine, and the damage was zero.

*Equipment strengthens valid answers; it never overrides immunity.*

## Skipping

Skipping is armed on the first request and confirmed on the second, so it
cannot be done by accident.
