# Workshop

The Workshop (**K**) is where items are improved rather than found. It runs on
**gold** and on materials recovered from things you no longer want.

## Materials

Salvaging yields reagents, each with its own role:

| Material | Used for |
|---|---|
| Forged Iron | General reforging |
| Reforging Dust | General reforging |
| Warded Leather | General reforging |
| Crystal Essence | Higher-tier operations |
| Powder of First Enchantment | Adding a stat |
| Powder of Erasure | Erasing a stat |
| Salt of Cleansing | Cleansing an item |
| Ichor of Empowerment | Strengthening |
| Ichor of Reshaping | Replacing a stat |
| Ichor of Sealing | Sealing a stat against change |
| Portent of Kinship | Set-related work |
| Portent of the Weighing | Weighted outcomes |
| Cursed Sigil | Invoked deliberately, at a price |

## Operations

**Add one stat** — fills an empty stat slot. Refused when every slot is full.

**Erase one stat** — removes a stat, freeing its slot.

**Replace one stat** — swaps one stat for another.

**Reroll values** — rerolls the numbers without changing which stats are
present.

**Reroll all stats** — a full reroll, the most expensive option.

**Cleanse item** — strips accumulated effects.

**Invoke Cursed Sigil** — a deliberate risk with its own payoff.

## Sealing

A sealed stat is protected from change. Once every stat on an item is sealed
the Workshop will tell you so plainly — *"Every stat is sealed"* — and refuse
further modification. Sealing is how you lock in a good roll before gambling on
the rest.

## Salvage

Unwanted items break down into materials. The Workshop shows the yield before
you commit, and valuable items ask for confirmation before they are destroyed.

Salvage is the intended source of reagents. An item you will never equip is not
waste; it is the input to the item you will.
