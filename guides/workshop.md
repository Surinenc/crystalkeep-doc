# Workshop

The Workshop (**K**) is where items are improved rather than found. It runs on
**gold** and on materials recovered from things you no longer want.

## Materials

Salvaging yields reagents, each with its own role:

| Material | Used for |
|---|---|
| Forged Iron | General reforging |
| Reforging Dust | General reforging |
| Warded Leather | General reforging |
| Crystal Essence | Higher-tier operations |
| Powder of First Enchantment | Adding a stat |
| Powder of Erasure | Erasing a stat |
| Salt of Cleansing | Cleansing an item |
| Ichor of Empowerment | Strengthening |
| Ichor of Reshaping | Replacing a stat |
| Ichor of Sealing | Sealing a stat against change |
| Portent of Kinship | Set-related work |
| Portent of the Weighing | Weighted outcomes |
| Salt of Renewal | Rerolling values |
| Cursed Sigil | Invoked deliberately, at a price |

## Operations

Each operation is bound to the material that performs it, and every one except
Cleansing respects seals.

| Operation | Material | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| **Seed first stat** | Powder of First Enchantment | Adds one random stat to an item with no ordinary stat lines |
| **Add one stat** | Ichor of Empowerment | Fills one empty slot without changing existing rolls |
| **Erase one stat** | Powder of Erasure | Removes one unsealed stat; a Weighing targets the weakest roll |
| **Replace one stat** | Ichor of Reshaping | Replaces one unsealed stat; Portents can aim the loss or shape the new family |
| **Reroll values** | Salt of Renewal | Rerolls every unsealed stat's value without changing which stats are present |
| **Seal one stat** | Ichor of Sealing | Permanently protects one stat from every operation **except Cleansing** |
| **Cleanse item** | Salt of Cleansing | Removes every ordinary stat **and every seal**, preserving the item |
| **Invoke Cursed Sigil** | Cursed Sigil | One final 25% roll: overcap a value, add a slot, bind the item, or destroy it |

The Ledger also offers **Reroll all stats** for gold, as a single expensive
operation rather than a material cost.

## Sealing, and the one thing that breaks it

A sealed stat is protected from every operation — except Cleansing, which
strips the seals along with the stats. That exception is the whole risk model:
sealing is how you bank a good roll while you gamble on the rest, and Cleansing
is how you undo the bank if you want a fresh start.

Once every stat is sealed the Workshop says so plainly — *"Every stat is
sealed"* — and refuses further modification.

The Cursed Sigil is the only operation that can destroy an item outright. It is
a 25% roll with four outcomes, one of which is losing the item.

## Salvage

Unwanted items break down into materials. The Workshop shows the yield before
you commit, and valuable items ask for confirmation before they are destroyed.

Salvage is the intended source of reagents. An item you will never equip is not
waste; it is the input to the item you will.
